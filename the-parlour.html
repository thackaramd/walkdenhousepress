THE PARLOUR

The Parlour is our literary magazine: a place for writing that feels alive, attentive, and grounded in lived experience.

It brings together work by writers of different ages and backgrounds, with an interest in the particular — in thought, feeling, memory, conversation, and the overlooked textures of everyday life. At its heart is a simple idea: that literature can be a place to meet and be met.
